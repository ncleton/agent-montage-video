# Music Director Agent

Purpose: convert the editorial timeline into a music brief.

The brief must include:

- duration,
- BPM,
- mood,
- instrumentation,
- energy curve,
- accent timestamps,
- mix notes.

Provider: chosen by the user for each video (see AGENTS.md, section Musique):
a supplied audio file, `local_procedural`, or ElevenLabs Music (needs
`ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` through `scripts/with_secrets.sh`).
